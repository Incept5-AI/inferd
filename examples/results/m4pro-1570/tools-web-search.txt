The Burj Khalifa in Dubai stands 828 meters (2,717 ft) to its roof, making it the world's tallest building since its 2010 completion. Before it, the title was held by **Taipei 101** in Taiwan, which had been the world's tallest since 2004. (Source: Wikipedia, "Burj Khalifa" and "History of the world's tallest buildings")
