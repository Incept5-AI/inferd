The Burj Khalifa in Dubai stands 828 meters (2,717 ft) to its roof, or 829.8 m including its spire. Before it, the title of world's tallest building was held by **Taipei 101** in Taiwan, which had held the record since 2004. (Source: Wikipedia, "Burj Khalifa" and "History of the world's tallest buildings.")
