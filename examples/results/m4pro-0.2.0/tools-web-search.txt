The Burj Khalifa in Dubai stands 828 meters (2,717 feet) tall, making it the world's tallest building since its completion in 2010. Before it, the title was held by **Taipei 101** in Taiwan, which had been the world's tallest building since 2004. (Source: Wikipedia, "Burj Khalifa" and "History of the world's tallest buildings")
